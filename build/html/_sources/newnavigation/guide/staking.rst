Staking ERB
=================

Enter the Miniapp Wallet through Erbie's Telegram Bot.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

`Open Erbie Chain Bot <https://t.me/ErbieChainBot>`_

.. image:: staker1.jpg

Click the Wallet button to open the app. If you don't have an account, please create a new one.
Please make sure to back up your mnemonic phrase and private key, as they are the only ways to recover your account.

.. image:: staker2.jpg

If you already have an account, please enter your password to log in.

.. image:: staker3.jpg

After entering the wallet, you will see your asset balance and various function buttons. Expand the function menu by clicking the arrow.

.. image:: staker4.jpg

Click the arrow and select "Builder" to enter the staking page.

.. image:: staker5.jpg

Click the arrow and select the matching Validator to stake.

.. image:: staker6.jpg

You can either enter the Validator address or select from the list.

.. image:: staker7.jpg

Ensure your account balance is greater than 350 ERB, and stake at least 350 ERB to become a Staker and participate in mining reward distribution.

For detailed distribution information,please see the following link:

`rewards <https://www.erbie.io/erbiedocs/newnavigation/learn/incentive/rewards.html>`_
